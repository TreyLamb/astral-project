    📋 Weekly routine for the Flight Commander (start here)
    1. Pick yourself as Flight Commander at the top of the slate.
    2. Fill the other seven roles. Green and blue cells are good picks; the warnings above tell you if a pick breaks a rule.
    3. When the week's roles are final, press Roll forward to next week. This week's slate becomes Wk-1, the old Wk-1 becomes Wk-2, and the slate, Flight Commander and “Must” boxes clear.
    4. Press Copy share link and send it to the next Flight Commander. The link carries the whole chart, so they open it exactly as you left it.

    Rules baked in: BCL-only roles are Deputy, Standardization, Academic and Drill & Ceremony. Same role as last week is blocked, last week's Flight Commander can't be Deputy, and the Flight Commander holds no other role. “Must” is for any cadet your leadership says has to be assigned this week. Captain and POCs aren't listed.
