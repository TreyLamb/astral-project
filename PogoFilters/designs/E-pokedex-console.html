Design E — Pokédex console (“Cardinal Glass”). Same glass-panel construction language as MedalDex and POGO Accs so it reads as a sibling, but a crimson/amethyst/amber palette of its own rather than a reskin. Tier buttons are physical illuminated console keys that light when engaged. Scanlines are a single overlay on the screen, not per row, and the only backdrop-filter is on the frozen header — so 900 rows still scroll at 60fps.
