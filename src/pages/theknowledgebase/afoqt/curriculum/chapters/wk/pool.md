# Extended word pool

**There is nothing to read here, and that is deliberate.**

This chapter is not instruction. It is a bank of high-confidence GRE-tier vocabulary whose only
job is to make Word Knowledge drills stop repeating themselves. Every word in it carries the same
structure as a taught word — a real definition, a genuine confusable, and four named wrong answers
— but no lesson walks you through them, because a lesson that marched through several hundred
headwords would be a list, not a lesson.

## How to use it

Drill it. `Drill → Word Knowledge` samples this pool alongside the taught chapters, and every word
in the subtest is equally likely to come up, so the way to meet these is simply to run drills.

Pick a **band** on the drill screen if you want to aim:

- **Band 3** — roughly where the real test sits.
- **Band 4** — the level worth owning. This is where most of your effort should go.
- **Band 5** — deliberately above the AFOQT. Depth, not pace.

A word you miss goes into your **word bank** automatically, and into the miss pool, so the ones you
actually get wrong come back on their own. That feedback loop is the study plan for this material —
not reading.

## Where these words come from

The candidate list is built from five curated hard GRE lists (Manhattan 1000, GregMat, Powerscore
Repeat Offenders, Magoosh Advanced, Barron's 333), scored by how many of them a word appears in,
with an easy-tier union subtracted. Words appearing in two or more of those lists are authored
first. Full sourcing: `docs/afoqt/WORD-BANK-EXPANSION.md`.

## What this chapter is not

It has no sequence and nothing to test out of. If you want vocabulary that is actually *taught* —
grouped by theme, with the confusable pairs explained and the traps named — that is chapters 5
through 13, and those are worth reading.
