# High-tier vocabulary III - temperament and mood

What a person is like, and how they feel. This chapter is chosen for the density of its near-identical pairs rather than for the words themselves - five of them differ by one or two letters from a common word that means something else entirely.

## How to attack this cluster

When a headword looks like a word you know, that is evidence you are being tested on
the difference, not the similarity. *Hapless* is not *helpless*; *restive* is not *restful*;
*histrionic* is not *historic*. Read the whole headword before you read any option.

## Seen in a sentence

- She was ebullient for a week after the checkride result came back.
- He took the emergency with an equanimity nobody else in the cockpit had.
- His phlegmatic manner made him the obvious choice for the night shift.
- The farewell turned lachrymose about an hour in.
- He delivered the weather brief in a lugubrious monotone.
- Two drinks in, the speech turned maudlin.
- The afternoon torpor of the crew room broke the moment the horn sounded.
- A week of night flying left the whole squadron in a state of lassitude.
- The turnout showed apathy rather than opposition.
- He made a fervid case for keeping the programme alive.
- The last ten minutes before launch were frenetic.
- His histrionic reaction to a routine write-up embarrassed everyone.
- She had a droll way of describing disasters that made them bearable.
- An imperious wave was the only acknowledgement anyone got.
- A complaisant crew chief agreed to every schedule change without argument.
- The obstreperous crowd drowned out the announcement entirely.
- The passengers grew restive after the third delay announcement.
- It was a quixotic plan that nobody expected to survive the budget review.
- His blithe assurance that it would all work out convinced nobody.
- A nettlesome fuel discrepancy held the whole flight on the ground.
- The hapless navigator drew the same broken aircraft three days running.
- Six months of alerts had inured him to the sound of the klaxon.
