# Atoms, electrons and the periodic trends

Four areas: the particles an atom is built from, what an electron does when its energy changes,
the trends that run across the periodic table, and radioactive decay.

---

## The three particles

| Particle | Charge | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Proton** | Positive | Nucleus |
| **Neutron** | None | Nucleus |
| **Electron** | Negative | Orbits the nucleus |

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Atomic number** | Number of protons — defines the element |
| **Mass number** | Protons **+** neutrons |
| **Isotope** | Same element, different neutron count than the common form |
| **Ion** | An atom that gained or lost electrons — has an overall charge |
| **Valence electron** | An outer-shell electron — does the chemical bonding |

> ⚠ **Atomic number is protons only. Mass number is protons plus neutrons.** A question that
> gives you both counts and asks for mass number is asking you to add them.

---

## Electron energy levels

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ground state** | An electron's lowest possible energy level |
| **Excited state** | Any level higher than ground state |
| **Absorption** | Electron **gains** energy, jumps to a *higher* level |
| **Emission** | Electron **releases** energy, falls to a *lower* level |
| **Photon** | A packet of light energy released during emission |
| **Quantum leap** | The abrupt jump itself — no state exists in between two levels |

> The direction is the whole test here: **up needs energy IN, down gives energy OUT.** An
> electron never drifts between levels — it jumps, and the jump is always discrete.

---

## Periodic trends

| Term | What it is | Trend across a period, left → right |
|---|---|---|
| **Atomic radius** | Size of the atom | Decreases |
| **Electronegativity** | Pull on shared bonding electrons | Increases |
| **Ionization energy** | Energy to remove an electron | Increases |

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Group** | A vertical column — similar chemical properties |
| **Period** | A horizontal row |
| **Metallic character** | How easily an atom gives up electrons — increases *down* a group |
| **Noble gas stability** | Group 18's low reactivity — outer shell already full |

> ⚠ **Radius shrinks while electronegativity and ionization energy both grow, moving the same
> direction across a period.** All three trends are driven by the same thing — more protons
> pulling the same-shell electrons in tighter — so they move together, not in opposite directions.

---

## Radioactivity and decay

| Type | Releases |
|---|---|
| **Alpha decay** | Two protons + two neutrons bound together |
| **Beta decay** | A fast electron, as a neutron converts to a proton |
| **Gamma decay** | Pure high-energy electromagnetic radiation, no mass at all |

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Half-life** | Time for half a radioactive sample to decay |
| **Nuclear fission** | Splitting a heavy nucleus into smaller ones |
| **Nuclear fusion** | Combining two light nuclei into a heavier one |
| **Decay chain** | The series of decays an unstable isotope goes through toward stability |

> ⚠ **Fission splits, fusion combines** — the two words already tell you which, but they are
> offered against each other constantly because they are visually similar.

---

## Before you move on

- [ ] Mass number = protons + neutrons, not protons alone.
- [ ] Absorption = energy in, moving up. Emission = energy out, moving down.
- [ ] Atomic radius shrinks while electronegativity and ionization energy both grow across a period.
- [ ] Fission splits a nucleus; fusion combines two.
