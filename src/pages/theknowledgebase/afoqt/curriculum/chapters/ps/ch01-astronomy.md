# The solar system and the sky

Physical Science is conceptual, almost no computation — name the thing, say what it does, in the
same register as the rest of Aviation Information's fact chapters. This chapter covers four areas:
the bodies of the solar system, Earth's own motion, eclipses and moon phases, and stars.

---

## Bodies of the solar system, smallest to biggest confusion

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Meteoroid** | A small rock or dust particle traveling through space |
| **Meteor** | The streak of light when a meteoroid burns up in the atmosphere |
| **Meteorite** | A meteoroid that survives the fall and actually lands |
| **Asteroid** | A rocky body, smaller than a planet, mostly in the asteroid belt |
| **Comet** | An icy body that grows a glowing tail near the Sun |
| **Planet** | Orbits a star, has cleared its orbital path of debris |
| **Dwarf planet** | Round, orbits the Sun, has **not** cleared its neighborhood (Pluto) |
| **Kuiper Belt** | The region beyond Neptune holding icy bodies and dwarf planets |

> ⚠ **Meteoroid → meteor → meteorite is a sequence, not three names for the same thing.** The
> object in space, the streak of light, and the surviving fragment on the ground are three
> different stages of one event.

## Earth's own motion

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Rotation** | Earth spinning on its axis, ~24 hours — gives day and night |
| **Revolution** | Earth's ~year-long orbit around the Sun |
| **Axial tilt** | The ~23.5° lean of Earth's axis — **the actual cause of the seasons** |
| **Solstice** | A hemisphere tilted most directly toward or away from the Sun |
| **Equinox** | Day and night roughly equal everywhere |
| **Perihelion / Aphelion** | Closest / farthest point in Earth's orbit from the Sun |

> The seasons are **not** caused by Earth being closer to or farther from the Sun over the year —
> that is perihelion/aphelion, a much smaller effect. Axial tilt is the real cause: it is why the
> Northern and Southern Hemispheres have opposite seasons at the same time.

## Eclipses and moon phases

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **New moon** | Moon between Earth and Sun, lit side away from us |
| **Full moon** | Earth between Sun and Moon, whole lit side visible |
| **Waxing** | More of the lit surface visible each night |
| **Waning** | Less of the lit surface visible each night |
| **Solar eclipse** | Moon passes between Sun and Earth, blocking sunlight |
| **Lunar eclipse** | Earth passes between Sun and Moon, casting its shadow on the Moon |
| **Umbra** | The dark, central part of a shadow — light fully blocked |
| **Penumbra** | The lighter, outer part of a shadow — light partly blocked |

> ⚠ **Solar and lunar eclipses are swapped constantly.** The test to keep them straight: which
> body is in the MIDDLE? Moon in the middle (blocking the Sun) = solar. Earth in the middle
> (shadowing the Moon) = lunar.

## Stars and the universe

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Star** | Massive ball of hot gas producing its own light via nuclear fusion |
| **Galaxy** | An enormous collection of stars, gas and dust held by gravity |
| **Milky Way** | The specific galaxy containing Earth's solar system |
| **Nebula** | A cloud of gas and dust — often where new stars form |
| **Light-year** | A unit of *distance* (how far light travels in a year) — not time |
| **Red giant** | A dying star, expanded and cooled after using its core hydrogen |
| **White dwarf** | The small, dense, cooling remnant of a low-mass star |
| **Supernova** | The explosive death of a massive star |

> ⚠ **A light-year measures distance, not time**, despite having "year" in the name. It is one of
> the most commonly misremembered facts in general-science material.

---

## Before you move on

- [ ] You can order meteoroid → meteor → meteorite without hesitating.
- [ ] Axial tilt causes seasons — not distance from the Sun.
- [ ] Solar eclipse: Moon in the middle. Lunar eclipse: Earth in the middle.
- [ ] A light-year is a distance, not a time.
