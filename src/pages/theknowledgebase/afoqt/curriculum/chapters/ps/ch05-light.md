# Light, reflection and the spectrum

Four areas: what a light wave's properties actually determine, reflection and refraction, lenses
and mirrors, and where visible light sits in the wider electromagnetic spectrum.

---

## Light wave properties

| Property | Determines |
|---|---|
| **Amplitude** | Brightness |
| **Frequency** | **Color** — and stays constant even when light enters a new medium |
| **Wavelength** | Changes when light enters a new medium (speed changes too) |

> ⚠ **Frequency determines color, not wavelength or speed.** When light passes into a denser
> medium, its speed and wavelength both change — but its frequency doesn't, which is exactly why
> the color you perceive doesn't change either. This is the single most counter-intuitive fact in
> the chapter, and it is tested directly.

Light also needs **no medium** to travel — it is an electromagnetic wave and crosses a vacuum,
unlike sound. It shows **wave-particle duality**: it behaves as both a wave and a stream of particles.

---

## Reflection and refraction

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Reflection** | Light bounces off a surface, travels in a new direction |
| **Refraction** | Light bends passing between media of different density |
| **Law of reflection** | Angle of incidence = angle of reflection, always |
| **Index of refraction** | How much a material slows and bends light |
| **Critical angle** | Beyond this angle, light stops refracting out of a denser medium at all |
| **Total internal reflection** | The result of exceeding the critical angle — light reflects entirely back in |
| **Dispersion** | White light splits into colors — each wavelength refracts differently |

## Lenses and mirrors

| Type | Curves | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Convex lens** | Outward | Converges light to a focal point |
| **Concave lens** | Inward | Diverges light |
| **Concave mirror** | Inward | Converges reflected light to a focal point |
| **Convex mirror** | Outward | Diverges reflected light — wider field of view |
| **Plane mirror** | Flat | Upright, same-size virtual image |

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Focal point** | Where parallel rays converge after a lens or curved mirror |
| **Real image** | Forms where light actually converges — can be projected on a screen |
| **Virtual image** | Cannot be projected (a plane mirror's image is virtual) |
| **Magnification** | Ratio of image size to actual object size |

> ⚠ **Convex and concave point the same way for lenses and mirrors: "-vex" bulges out and
> converges; "-cave" caves in and diverges** (for a lens) or converges (for a mirror, since
> reflection flips the geometry). Learn convex-lens and concave-mirror as the two that converge.

## The electromagnetic spectrum

Ordered **longest to shortest wavelength** (and therefore lowest to highest energy — the two run
inversely):

**Radio waves → Microwaves → Infrared → Visible light → Ultraviolet → X-rays → Gamma rays**

| Type | Where it sits | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| **Radio waves** | Longest wavelength | Broadcast signals |
| **Infrared** | Just past red, visible light | Felt as heat |
| **Ultraviolet** | Just past violet, visible light | Causes sunburn |
| **X-rays** | Short wavelength | Passes through soft tissue — medical imaging |
| **Gamma rays** | Shortest wavelength | Highest energy in the entire spectrum |

---

## Before you move on

- [ ] Frequency determines color; wavelength and speed both change in a new medium, frequency doesn't.
- [ ] Light needs no medium; sound does.
- [ ] Convex lens and concave mirror both converge light to a focal point.
- [ ] The spectrum runs radio → microwave → infrared → visible → ultraviolet → X-ray → gamma, shortest wavelength = highest energy.
