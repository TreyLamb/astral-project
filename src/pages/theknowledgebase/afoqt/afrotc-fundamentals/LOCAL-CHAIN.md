# Local Cadet Wing Chain — Detachment Routing

Supplied by the cadet, 2026-09-03, transcribed from an **Operations Order (OPORD)
coordination and approval routing sheet**. Fills the gap the public research could not:
per-detachment cadet billets are not published anywhere public.

⚠️ **DATED — VERIFY BEFORE RECITING.** Every signature on this sheet is dated
**9–18 March 2026**, i.e. the spring term. Cadet Wing billets normally rotate at least
annually, often each semester. It is now **September 2026 — a new academic year**, so
several of these names have likely changed. Treat this as the *structure* (which is
stable) plus a *probably-stale roster* (which is not). Confirm current names with cadre
or the Cadet Wing before a graded recitation.

## What this confirms

- **C/Col Laird is the Cadet Wing Commander** — the senior cadet, and the top of the
  cadet portion of the chain.
- **Maj Smith is the Operations Flight Commander**, an active-duty cadre officer. He is
  the final approver, which confirms the research's generic model: the Cadet Wing
  Commander reports to the OFC, and the OFC is the first *commissioned, active-duty*
  link above the cadet wing.
- The A-staff structure is live here — **A3 (Operations)** and **A4/5 (Logistics &
  Plans)** both sit in the approval path, alongside **CW/FM** (Financial Management) and
  **CW/DS** (Director of Staff).
- `CC` = Commander, `CD` = Deputy Commander throughout. `Coord` = coordinates (concurs);
  `Appr` = approves.

## Why this doubles as SOB 8.3 material

This sheet *is* the artifact SOB 8.3 grades. The POC-In-Charge Evaluation Form's
**PLANNING** criterion asks whether the cadet "considers Cadet Wing deadlines, develops
thorough documentation (Operations Orders, trainer info packages, Risk Management plan),
and gets the plan approved **in advance of the event**." This routing sheet is the
evidence of exactly that.

Note the **nine-day lead time** (09 Mar → 18 Mar) and that the POCIC signs **first** —
the project officer originates the OPORD, then walks it up through coordination
(flight → squadron), then approval (wing staff → CW/CC → cadre). Being able to describe
that flow is a direct answer to a "how do you plan and execute an event as POC-In-Charge"
question.
