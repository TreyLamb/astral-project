## 🚨 READ THIS FIRST — TWO PREMISES IN THE TASKING ARE WRONG

**🚨 FLAG 1 — There is NO "wreath on the Senior/Chief tiers" in Air Force enlisted insignia.**
Regular Senior Master Sergeant (E-8) and Chief Master Sergeant (E-9) insignia have **no wreath**. A wreath appears on **exactly one** USAF enlisted insignia: the **Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force (CMSAF)**, which has a **laurel wreath encircling the star** in the lower blue field. Wreaths on E-8/E-9 are an **Army / Marine Corps / Navy** convention, not USAF. If a test answer says "SMSgt has a wreath," it is wrong.

**🚨 FLAG 2 — AFROTC cadets do NOT hold "Cadet Airman Basic through Cadet Colonel."**
AFROTCI 36-2011 Vol 3, *Cadet Operations* (13 January 2025), para 10.7.5 states verbatim:

> "No cadet is authorized to hold **'enlisted' cadet rank** or any grade above Cadet Colonel (C/Col)."

AFROTC has **exactly 8 cadet grades**: C/4C, C/3C, then C/2d Lt → C/Col. Cadet enlisted ranks (C/AB, C/Amn, C/SSgt, etc.) belong to **AFJROTC** (high school) and **Civil Air Patrol** — *not* college AFROTC. Do not memorize a cadet enlisted ladder for this test.

# A) DAF ENLISTED — U.S. AIR FORCE (E-1 to E-9)

## A.1 How the USAF chevron is built (the mental model that makes all 9 memorizable)

The 1948 design is "a center circle encompassing a star, with wing-like stripes swept upward" (Air & Space Forces Magazine, *A Study in Stripes*). Read every USAF stripe in three parts:

1. **UPPER CHEVRONS** — the stripes **above** the star. Build up 1 → 2 → 3 for E-2/E-3/E-4, freeze at 3 through E-7, then **add a 4th at E-8 and a 5th at E-9**.
2. **THE STAR** — a **white/silver star inside the center circle**, present on every insignia E-2 through E-9. (Stars were changed from silver to **white** effective **October 1991**.)
3. **LOWER ROCKERS** — the curved stripes **below** the star. None for E-2/E-3/E-4; add 1 at E-5, 2 at E-6, **freeze at 3 from E-7 onward**.

**Total stripes = pay grade minus 1.** E-2 = 1 stripe … E-9 = 8 stripes. If your count doesn't equal (E-number − 1), you mis-drew it.

| Grade | Upper chevrons | Star | Lower rockers | TOTAL stripes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-1 | — | — | — | 0 (no insignia) |
| E-2 | 1 | ✔ | 0 | 1 |
| E-3 | 2 | ✔ | 0 | 2 |
| E-4 | 3 | ✔ | 0 | 3 |
| E-5 | 3 | ✔ | **1** | 4 |
| E-6 | 3 | ✔ | **2** | 5 |
| E-7 | 3 | ✔ | **3** | 6 |
| E-8 | **4** | ✔ | 3 | 7 |
| E-9 | **5** | ✔ | 3 | 8 |

*Cross-checked against two independent sources that agree on the E-7 anchor: Air & Space Forces Magazine describes E-7 Master Sergeant as "**three upper and three rocker stripes**," E-8 as MSgt "enhanced with an additional stripe," E-9 as MSgt plus two additional stripes; Wikipedia's USAF enlisted rank insignia article gives the same 3+3 / 4+3 / 5+3 breakdown. Colors: silver/white stripes on a **blue** field for dress; subdued for OCP.*

## A.2 Full USAF enlisted table

| Pay grade | Full title | Abbrev | Tier | Insignia description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E-1** | Airman Basic | **AB** | Airman | **No insignia.** (Only DAF grade with none.) |
| **E-2** | Airman | **Amn** | Airman | 1 chevron above a white star in the center circle. |
| **E-3** | Airman First Class | **A1C** | Airman | 2 chevrons above the star. |
| **E-4** | Senior Airman | **SrA** | Airman | 3 chevrons above the star. **Highest Airman-tier grade — still NOT an NCO.** |
| **E-5** | Staff Sergeant | **SSgt** | **NCO (first NCO grade)** | 3 chevrons above the star + **1 rocker** below. *Only NCO/SNCO grade with a single rocker.* |
| **E-6** | Technical Sergeant | **TSgt** | NCO | 3 chevrons above the star + **2 rockers** below. |
| **E-7** | Master Sergeant | **MSgt** | **SNCO (first SNCO grade)** | 3 chevrons above the star + **3 rockers** below. |
| **E-8** | Senior Master Sergeant | **SMSgt** | SNCO | **4** chevrons above the star + 3 rockers below. |
| **E-9** | Chief Master Sergeant | **CMSgt** | SNCO | **5** chevrons above the star + 3 rockers below. |

### Tier boundaries — the single highest-yield fact set

- **Airman tier = E-1 to E-4** (AB, Amn, A1C, SrA)
- **NCO tier = E-5 to E-6** (SSgt, TSgt) → **first NCO is E-5 Staff Sergeant**
- **SNCO tier = E-7 to E-9** (MSgt, SMSgt, CMSgt) → **first SNCO is E-7 Master Sergeant**
- The Air Force is the **outlier**: in the Army/Marines E-4 (Corporal) is already an NCO. In the USAF/USSF, **E-4 is not an NCO.**

## A.3 Special positions and duty insignia

| Position | Grades held | Insignia |
|---|---|---|
| **First Sergeant** (a *duty position/special duty identifier*, **not a rank**) | E-7, E-8, E-9 | Identical chevron for that grade, but a **diamond (a "lozenge") replaces the star** in the center circle. So there are three distinct 1st Sgt insignia: MSgt/1st Sgt (3+3), SMSgt/1st Sgt (4+3), CMSgt/1st Sgt (5+3). |
| **Command Chief Master Sergeant (CCM)** | E-9 | Standard CMSgt chevron (5 up / star / 3 rockers) with **an additional star added in the upper field**, between the top chevrons. |
| **Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force (CMSAF)** | E-9, one per service | 8 stripes (5 up / 3 rockers), with a **laurel wreath encircling the star** in the lower blue field, plus the **Great Seal of the United States flanked by two stars in the upper field**. The Great Seal + upper stars were added **1 November 2004**; the wreath-and-star is the legacy element retained from the original stripe. |
| **Senior Enlisted Advisor to the Chairman (SEAC)** | E-9, joint | Unique joint insignia (design established Dec 2019). Low test yield for a GMC cadet — know the acronym only. |

## A.4 🚨 CRITICAL VERIFICATION — E-4 status and 2025–2026 changes

- **"Senior Airman" is STILL the E-4 title.** Verified current as of 2026: military reporting from **August 2026** describes "the Air Force promot[ing] over 12,500 **senior airmen** to staff sergeant." No E-4 retitling has occurred. **No change** to the E-1→E-9 titles for 2025 or 2026 was found in any source.
- **No 2025–2026 DAF enlisted force-structure change** to the grade ladder was found. Historical footnote only: E-4 briefly split into "Senior Airman" and "Sergeant" (buck sergeant) from 1976–1991; the star went from blue to **white** in Oct 1991. That is history, not current.

### Warrant Officers — current status (W-1 to W-5)

**Warrant officers are BACK in the Air Force, and they now appear on the official DAF rank/insignia chart.**

| Grade | Title | Abbrev |
|---|---|---|
| W-1 | Warrant Officer 1 | **WO1 / W-1** |
| W-2 | Chief Warrant Officer 2 | **CW2 / CW-2** |
| W-3 | Chief Warrant Officer 3 | **CW3 / CW-3** |
| W-4 | Chief Warrant Officer 4 | **CW4 / CW-4** |
| W-5 | Chief Warrant Officer 5 | **CW5 / CW-5** |

- **Where they sit:** above **all** enlisted (including CMSAF) and below **all** commissioned officers (below O-1). W-1 is appointed by warrant; CW-2 through CW-5 are **commissioned**.
- **Career fields:** limited to **cyber and IT only** — AFSC **17W** (Warfighter Communications & IT Systems Operations) and **17Y** (Cyber Effects & Warfare Operations).
- **Timeline:** applications opened Feb 2024; **Warrant Officer Training School** activated at Maxwell AFB (Holm Center); **first class graduated 6 Dec 2024** — the first USAF warrant officers in **66 years**. Corps was ~200 strong as of 2025–2026, and **CW5 Jason Godwin** was named the service's senior warrant officer in **January 2026**.
- **Space Force does NOT use warrant officers.**
- **Insignia:** silver/blue segmented bars (blue enamel squares on a silver bar), the pattern varying by grade — the classic pre-1959 USAF warrant device style.
- **🚨 Should a cadet memorize this?** **Know that it exists, its two career fields, and that WOs slot between E-9 and O-1.** The LLAB objective is worded "officer grade structure," and warrant officers now appear on the *Rank Insignia of the USAF & USSF* chart, so a detachment could ask. But **priority is far below E-1→E-9 and O-1→O-10.** Learn it last.

# B) DAF ENLISTED — U.S. SPACE FORCE (E-1 to E-9)

Space Force enlisted rank **names** were released **February 2021**; Space Force enlisted **insignia** were unveiled **20–21 September 2021**. Guardians wore USAF insignia until the USSF designs were approved.

| Pay grade | USSF title | Abbrev | Term of address (official) | Tier | Insignia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E-1** | Specialist 1 | **Spc1** | "Specialist" (Spec1 / Specialist 1 acceptable) | Specialist | **Dark blue hexagon** (6 sides = 6th branch) with a **white delta** in the middle. **No stripe.** |
| **E-2** | Specialist 2 | **Spc2** | "Specialist" | Specialist | Hexagon + delta + **1 horizontal "Vandenberg stripe"** across the delta. |
| **E-3** | Specialist 3 | **Spc3** | "Specialist" | Specialist | Hexagon + delta + **2** horizontal stripes. |
| **E-4** | Specialist 4 | **Spc4** | "Specialist" | Specialist | Hexagon + delta + **3** horizontal stripes. |
| **E-5** | Sergeant | **Sgt** | "Sergeant" | **NCO (first NCO)** | **3 upward-bent chevrons** with the **Delta, Globe and Orbit** device above. |
| **E-6** | Technical Sergeant | **TSgt** | "Sergeant" / "Technical Sergeant" / "Tech Sergeant" | NCO | **4** upward-bent chevrons + Delta, Globe and Orbit. |
| **E-7** | Master Sergeant | **MSgt** | "Sergeant" / "Master Sergeant" | **SNCO (first SNCO)** | Delta ascending over the globe with **1 arched "orbital" chevron** (= Low Earth Orbit). |
| **E-8** | Senior Master Sergeant | **SMSgt** | "Senior" / "Senior Master Sergeant" | SNCO | Delta over globe with **2** orbital chevrons (Low + Medium Earth Orbit). |
| **E-9** | Chief Master Sergeant | **CMSgt** | "Chief" / "Chief Master Sergeant" | SNCO | Delta over globe with **3** orbital chevrons (Low + Medium Earth + Geosynchronous Orbit). |
| **E-9** | Chief Master Sergeant of the **Space Force** | **CMSSF** | "Chief" / "Chief Master Sergeant of the Space Force" | SNCO | CMSgt design **plus stars and braiding** around the Delta, Globe and Orbit, with the delta set over the globe. |

**Which service uses which — say it out loud:**
- **Air Force E-1 to E-4** = Airman Basic / Airman / Airman First Class / Senior Airman.
- **Space Force E-1 to E-4** = Specialist 1 / 2 / 3 / 4.
- **From E-6 up the titles are IDENTICAL** (Technical, Master, Senior Master, Chief Master Sergeant).
- **The ONLY divergence at NCO level: E-5.** Air Force = **Staff Sergeant (SSgt)**. Space Force = **Sergeant (Sgt)**. *There is no USSF Staff Sergeant.* This is a classic trap question.
- Tier names are the same structure: **Specialist (E-1–E-4) / NCO (E-5–E-6) / SNCO (E-7–E-9)**.
- **Space Force officer titles are IDENTICAL to Air Force** (2d Lt → General) — see Section C.
- Space Force members are **Guardians**; Air Force members are **Airmen**.

**🚨 FLAG — unverified for USSF:** whether the Space Force fields a **First Sergeant (diamond)** duty position or a **Command Chief Master Sergeant** insignia variant. The official *Rank Insignia of the USAF & USSF* chart (AFA Almanac 2025) shows **First Sergeant** and **Command Chief Master Sergeant** on the **Air Force** side only; the Space Force side lists only CMSgt and CMSSF. Secondary sources conflict. **Assume First Sergeant/Command Chief = Air Force answers unless your detachment chart says otherwise.**

# C) OFFICER GRADE STRUCTURE — AIR FORCE **and** SPACE FORCE (O-1 to O-10)

**USAF and USSF officer titles, abbreviations, and insignia are 100% identical.** One table serves both.

| Pay grade | Title | Abbrev | Grouping | Insignia |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O-1** | Second Lieutenant | **2d Lt** | Company Grade (CGO) | **One GOLD bar** ("butter bar") |
| **O-2** | First Lieutenant | **1st Lt** | Company Grade (CGO) | **One SILVER bar** |
| **O-3** | Captain | **Capt** | Company Grade (CGO) | **Two SILVER bars** (connected; "railroad tracks") |
| **O-4** | Major | **Maj** | Field Grade (FGO) | **GOLD oak leaf** |
| **O-5** | Lieutenant Colonel | **Lt Col** | Field Grade (FGO) | **SILVER oak leaf** |
| **O-6** | Colonel | **Col** | Field Grade (FGO) | **SILVER eagle** (facing the wearer's right / toward the eagle's own right, holding arrows and olive branch) |
| **O-7** | Brigadier General | **Brig Gen** | General Officer | **1 silver star** |
| **O-8** | Major General | **Maj Gen** | General Officer | **2 silver stars** |
| **O-9** | Lieutenant General | **Lt Gen** | General Officer | **3 silver stars** |
| **O-10** | General | **Gen** | General Officer | **4 silver stars** |
| *(O-11, wartime only)* | *General of the Air Force* | *GAF* | — | *5 stars in a pentagon. Held by exactly one person ever: **Henry H. "Hap" Arnold**. Not currently held.* |

### 🔑 The gold-before-silver rule (classic test question)

> **Within each pair, GOLD is JUNIOR and SILVER is SENIOR.**
> - **2d Lt = GOLD bar** → **1st Lt = SILVER bar**
> - **Major = GOLD oak leaf** → **Lt Col = SILVER oak leaf**

Memory hook: *"Gold is cheaper than silver in the military."* Also: **a 2d Lt and a Major are the two gold-wearers; a 1st Lt and a Lt Col are the two silver-wearers.**

**Other high-yield officer traps**
- **1st Lt (silver bar) and Captain (two silver bars)** are the same metal — Captain just has two.
- **Colonel's eagle is silver.** There is no gold eagle in the DAF.
- **All general officer stars are silver.**
- **Terms of address:** all four general grades are addressed simply as **"General."** Both lieutenant grades are addressed as **"Lieutenant."** **Lt Col is addressed as "Colonel."** (Official DAF terms-of-address chart.)
- Groupings: **CGO = O-1 to O-3; FGO = O-4 to O-6; General Officer = O-7 to O-10.**

*The Coast Guard officer ladder is identical to the Navy's. Note the Coast Guard is in the Department of Homeland Security in peacetime.*

### 🔑 The Navy/Coast Guard traps — memorize these five

1. **Navy O-3 "Lieutenant" = Air Force CAPTAIN.** A Navy Lieutenant is *not* a Lieutenant-equivalent.
2. **Navy O-6 "Captain" = Air Force COLONEL.** A Navy Captain is *three grades senior* to an Air Force Captain. This is the #1 cross-service question.
3. **Navy O-1 is "Ensign," not "Second Lieutenant."** There is no Navy/CG "lieutenant colonel," "major," or "general."
4. **Navy O-7 vs O-8:** *both* are called "Rear Admiral." **Lower Half = RDML = O-7 (one star); Upper Half = RADM = O-8 (two stars).** The Navy has **no** one-word "Brigadier"-equivalent.
5. **Navy/CG flag officers are "Admirals," never "Generals."** ADM (O-10) = GEN (O-10).

### Joint insignia — the part that makes the table easy

On **collar/khaki/shoulder devices, the metal insignia are the SAME across all six services for O-1 through O-10.** Only the *titles* change.

| Device | AF/SF/Army/USMC | Navy/Coast Guard |
|---|---|---|
| One **gold** bar | 2d Lt (O-1) | Ensign (O-1) |
| One **silver** bar | 1st Lt (O-2) | LTJG (O-2) |
| Two **silver** bars | Captain (O-3) | Lieutenant (O-3) |
| **Gold** oak leaf | Major (O-4) | Lieutenant Commander (O-4) |
| **Silver** oak leaf | Lt Col (O-5) | Commander (O-5) |
| **Silver** eagle | Colonel (O-6) | Captain (O-6) |
| 1–4 **silver** stars | Brig Gen → General | RDML → Admiral |

*Navy/CG additionally wear **gold sleeve stripes** on dress blues and gold-striped shoulder boards — a system the ground/air services do not use. On working/khaki uniforms the Navy uses the same pin-on devices as the Marine Corps.*

**Warrant officers across the services:** Army, Marine Corps, Navy and (since 2024) Air Force use warrant grades. **Coast Guard does not use W-1 or W-5** (CWO2–CWO4 only). **Space Force uses no warrant officers.**

# E) AFROTC CADET GRADE STRUCTURE

## E.1 GMC vs POC — the definitions

| Term | Meaning | Classes | Cadet grades authorized |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GMC** — **General Military Course** | The first two years of the AFROTC program (freshman + sophomore). Non-contract or contract cadets who have **not yet completed Field Training**. | **AS100** (freshman/1st yr) and **AS200** (sophomore/2nd yr) + LLAB | **C/4C** (AS100) and **C/3C** (AS200) only |
| **POC** — **Professional Officer Course** | The last two years (junior + senior). Entered **only after successfully completing (or being properly deferred from) Field Training (FT)**. POC cadets run the cadet wing. | **AS300** (junior/3rd yr) and **AS400** (senior/4th yr) + LLAB | **Cadet officer grades C/2d Lt through C/Col** |

**The gate between GMC and POC is FIELD TRAINING.** Per AFROTCI 36-2011 V3 para 10.7.5.2, POC ranks are open only to "POC cadets who have successfully completed or have been properly deferred from FT."

Other AS designators you may see: **AS250** (concurrently enrolled / 2-year GMC — wears C/4C first term, C/3C second term), **AS500** (extended/retained), **AS700** (extended POC).

## E.3 Wear rules cadets get quizzed on (verbatim from DAFI 36-2903_AFROTCSUP)

- **Blues / outerwear (metallic rank on epaulets):** "Rank will be centered on both epaulets **5/8ths-inch from the shoulder seam**. **POC cadets will wear metallic black and silver** cadet officer rank insignia **with the silver stripe(s) parallel to the shoulder seam**. **GMC cadets will wear metallic blue and silver** rank insignia **with the point toward the neck**."
- **Service dress / long- and short-sleeve shirts / sweater:** **cloth shoulder marks** slide onto the epaulets and are buttoned; the bottom edge sits as close as possible to the upper sleeve seam.
- **OCP (Velcro rank on the chest tab):** **POC** cadets center the rank with the **silver stripe(s) horizontal**; **GMC** cadets center it with the short edge parallel to the bottom of the Velcro square and the **chevrons pointing upward**.
- **Rank at Field Training:** *every* cadet wears **C/3C** at FT, regardless of detachment rank. GMC cadets also wear **C/3C** at any Special Program or PMT.
- **Cadet Training Assistants (CTAs)** wear **C/Col** rank insignia while serving at FT.
- **Headgear:** **POC cadets wear the "Prop and Wings"** flight cap insignia. (GMC cadets do not.)
- **Mess dress:** **POC cadets wear silver sleeve braid.**
- **"Cadet" must precede any reference to the rank** (AFROTCI 36-2011 V3, 10.7.5) — it is *Cadet Captain Smith*, never *Captain Smith*.
- Cadet ranks "must be commensurate with their AS level and their assigned positions within the cadet wing," POC positions rotate at least once per academic year, and **no cadet may hold a grade above C/Col**.
- **USSF-select cadets** wear **Space Blue** OCP nametapes and a "U.S. Space Force" tape; USAF cadets wear **Spice Brown**.

# MOST LIKELY TEST QUESTIONS (highest-yield memorization)

**Tier boundaries**
1. **First NCO grade = E-5, Staff Sergeant (SSgt)** — Air Force. In the **Space Force** it is E-5, **Sergeant (Sgt)**.
2. **First SNCO grade = E-7, Master Sergeant (MSgt).**
3. **Airman tier = E-1 to E-4. E-4 Senior Airman is NOT an NCO** (unlike the Army/Marine E-4 Corporal).
4. **E-1 Airman Basic is the only grade with no insignia.**

**Insignia counting**
5. **Total stripes = pay grade − 1.** CMSgt = 8 stripes.
6. **E-4 SrA = 3 chevrons, no rockers. E-5 SSgt = 3 chevrons + 1 rocker.** The difference between SrA and SSgt is *one rocker added below the star*.
7. **Rockers stop at 3 (E-7). Then stripes are added ON TOP: E-8 = 4 up, E-9 = 5 up.**
8. **First Sergeant = a diamond replaces the star.** It is a **duty position**, held at **E-7, E-8, or E-9** — not a rank.
9. **Command Chief (E-9) = CMSgt chevron + an extra star in the upper field.**
10. **CMSAF = the only USAF enlisted insignia with a laurel wreath**, plus the Great Seal and two stars in the upper field.

**Officer gold vs silver**
11. **2d Lt = GOLD bar; 1st Lt = SILVER bar. Major = GOLD oak leaf; Lt Col = SILVER oak leaf.** Gold is always the *junior* of the pair.
12. **Colonel = silver eagle. Captain = two silver bars.**
13. **CGO = O-1 to O-3; FGO = O-4 to O-6; General Officer = O-7 to O-10.**
14. **Brig Gen 1 star / Maj Gen 2 / Lt Gen 3 / Gen 4.** All silver.

**Joint equivalence**
15. **Navy O-3 Lieutenant (LT) = Air Force Captain.**
16. **Navy O-6 Captain (CAPT) = Air Force Colonel.**
17. **Navy O-1 = Ensign; O-2 = Lieutenant (Junior Grade), LTJG.**
18. **Navy O-7 = Rear Admiral (Lower Half), RDML (1 star); O-8 = Rear Admiral (Upper Half), RADM (2 stars).**
19. **Coast Guard officer ranks = Navy officer ranks, exactly.**
20. **Collar devices are identical across services** — a gold bar is an O-1 in every service.

**DAF split**
21. **USAF E-1→E-4 = AB / Amn / A1C / SrA. USSF E-1→E-4 = Specialist 1 / 2 / 3 / 4.**
22. **USAF E-5 = Staff Sergeant. USSF E-5 = Sergeant.** (No USSF "Staff Sergeant.")
23. **USSF officer ranks = USAF officer ranks, identical.** Senior enlisted = **CMSSF**, vs **CMSAF** for the Air Force.
24. **Space Force members = Guardians. USSF hexagon = 6th branch of the Armed Forces.**

**AFROTC cadet**
25. **GMC = AS100/AS200; POC = AS300/AS400. Field Training is the gate between them.**
26. **AFROTC has 8 cadet grades: C/4C, C/3C, C/2d Lt, C/1st Lt, C/Capt, C/Maj, C/Lt Col, C/Col.** **No cadet enlisted ranks.**
27. **GMC insignia = blue and silver (point toward the neck). POC insignia = black and silver (stripes parallel to the shoulder seam).**
28. **C/4C = 1 stripe; C/3C = 2 stripes.**
29. **Cadet Colonel = the highest cadet grade = normally the Cadet Wing Commander.**
30. **Everyone wears C/3C at Field Training.**

## 🚨 MEDIUM CONFIDENCE — VERIFY AGAINST YOUR DETACHMENT'S ISSUED RANK CHART

1. **🚨 AFROTC cadet officer stripe patterns (C/2d Lt through C/Col).** The official pattern is in **Figure A2.4 of DAFI 36-2903_AFROTCSUP** and **Figures 10.1/10.2 of AFROTCI 36-2008** — but those are **images embedded in the PDFs and could not be machine-read**. The stripe counts in Section E.2 (1 wide / 1 wide+1 narrow / 2 wide / 2 wide+1 narrow between / 3 wide / 4 wide) come from cadet study material and vendor descriptions that agree with each other, **not from a text statement in the pub**. The *grade names, abbreviations, order, and black-and-silver color* ARE pub-confirmed. **Confirm the exact stripe pattern visually against your detachment's issued rank guide before the test.**
2. **🚨 C/4C and C/3C stripe orientation.** The pub confirms **blue and silver** with "the point toward the neck." Whether the silver element reads as a diagonal stripe or a chevron depends on how the epaulet is oriented; secondary sources describe C/4C as **1 stripe** and C/3C as **2 stripes**. Count is well supported; the descriptive wording is not pub-verbatim.
3. **🚨 Whether the Space Force fields First Sergeants or a Command Chief insignia variant.** Sources conflict; the official USAF/USSF chart shows both only on the Air Force side. Treat First Sergeant and Command Chief as **Air Force** answers.
4. **🚨 Air Force warrant officer insignia artwork.** Described as silver bars with blue enamel segments varying by grade, from vendor/collector listings — **not from a current DAF publication I could read**. The *grades* (W-1, CW-2, CW-3, CW-4, CW-5) are on the official chart.
