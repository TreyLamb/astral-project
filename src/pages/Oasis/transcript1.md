Project/Goal Overview
Establish a streamlined system to integrate daily logistics, health goals (blood donation/testosterone management), and AI-assisted research into a single, organized workflow.

Task List

Daily Planner Optimization: Update calendar to include hygiene (hair, cleaning), nutrition/meal planning, and "Pokemon goals".


Calendar Integration: Ensure meal times are mapped alongside daily events to visualize and prevent scheduling conflicts.


Gemini Workflow Implementation: Finalize the use of voice transcripts fed into Gemini to generate ready-to-use markdown notes for PC-based organization.


System Training: Train Gemini to process brief prompts into well-researched, structured reports without requiring manual cleanup.


Health Transition Plan: Create a schedule to taper off testosterone prior to Basic Training while managing cardio/respiratory performance.

Decision Log

Workflow Method: Decided to utilize voice transcripts for processing, as it is more efficient, trustworthy, and reduces the need to monitor real-time AI responses on mobile.

Open Research/Questions

Blood Donation Logistics: What are the specific health and recovery limitations regarding working out after donating blood or plasma?


Donation Frequency: How often can/should blood be donated while maintaining a high-intensity training regimen?


Automation: Can a script be developed to batch-process multiple transcripts simultaneously, or is manual entry required?
