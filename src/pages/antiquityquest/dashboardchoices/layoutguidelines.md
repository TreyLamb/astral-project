Product Requirements Document (PRD): Virtual Dashboard Layout
1. Table & Spatial Arrangement
Orbital Seating Structure: The interface must maintain a traditional, physical "round table" or elliptical feel. Player zones are distributed along the perimeter of the screen looking inward.

Empty Central Focus: There is no shared center deck, discard pile, or active tracker in the middle of the board. The central real estate must remain completely open and clean.

Dynamic Scaling Profiles: The board framework must dynamically shift its perimeter seating arrangement based on player count:

4-Player Mode: A strict cross configuration (1 Top, 1 Bottom, 1 Left, 1 Right).

6-Player Mode: An elliptical configuration (2 Top, 2 Bottom, 1 Left, 1 Right).

8-Player Mode: An optimized oval layout (3 Top, 3 Bottom, 1 Left, 1 Right).

Inward Center Shift: The lateral flank players and the bottom rows must sit shifted slightly inward toward the center vertical axis rather than hugging the screen edges, naturally anchoring the table's circular shape.

2. Element Orientation & Text Rules
Absolute Front-Facing View (0° Rotation): Every single graphical element on the screen must face the viewer directly.

No Peripheral Tilting: Player names, point values, asset descriptions, and card text for the left, right, and top positions must not be rotated vertically or upside down. All text paths must read normally from left to right, up to down.

3. High-Density Card Pooling & Flow
Mass Stacking Capacity: Each individual player zone must be designed to comfortably hold and display high-density card pools ranging from 20 to 30 active cards simultaneously without causing UI clutter.

Header-First Layout: The player's name tag and profile info must sit strictly at the top of their specific territory, acting as an anchor.

Downward Vertical Cascade: Card melds must form overlapping vertical chains that stack and flow downward away from the player name header, cascading naturally toward the outer frame margins.

4. HUD & Interface Scoring
Corner Leaderboard: The top-left corner of the screen must feature a dedicated, minimalist HUD panel displaying current standings.

Strict 2xN Grid Matrix: This scoreboard must be a clean table format featuring exactly 2 columns or 2 rows (Player Name | Score) and a dynamic number of rows/columns corresponding precisely to the player count (e.g., 2x4, 2x6, or 2x8).

5. Visual Identity & Environmental Zoning
Saturated Environmental Blending: The dashboard must utilize highly saturated, high-pop neon radial gradients to color-code and define each player's active territory.

No Rigid Borders: Territories must blend organically. avoid the need for hard solid divider lines.
