# Weekly Warrior Knowledge Quizzes — study guide

Det 855. The questions and answer options below are copied **word for word** from the two Google
Forms Trey posted on 2026-09-25 (Week 1 and Week 2). **The forms do not show an answer key**, so
every marked answer is one we worked out, not one the quiz told us.

**How to read the marks**
- ✅ **Sure** — matches a source we checked (AFROTCI 36-2011, the Det 432 handbook, Det 855's own
  uniform guide, or the repo's own recitation sheet).
- ⚠️ **Best guess** — the answer is inferred, not found written down anywhere. Treat it as
  unconfirmed until cadre or a syllabus says otherwise. These are flagged on purpose rather than
  presented as fact.

Only Weeks 1 and 2 are here. Add later weeks at the bottom, in the same format.

## Week 1

**1. Which of the following is an officer grade in the U.S. Air Force?**
- Ensign
- Admiral
- **Captain** ✅
- Senior Airman

> Ensign and Admiral are Navy. Senior Airman is enlisted.

**2. Which rank is the highest enlisted grade in the Air Force?**
- Senior Master Sergeant
- Chief Master Sergeant
- **Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force** ✅
- Master Sergeant

> The CMSAF is one unique position, senior to every other E-9 chief.

**3. Which sequence correctly represents the top of the military chain of command?**
- Secretary of Defense → President → Secretary of the Air Force → CSAF
- **President → Secretary of Defense → Secretary of the Air Force → Chief of Staff of the Air Force** ✅
- President → Secretary of the Air Force → Secretary of Defense → CSAF
- President → Chief of Staff of the Air Force → Secretary of Defense → Secretary of the Air Force

**4. Who is currently the Chief of Staff of the Air Force?**
- **Gen. Kenneth S. Wilsbach** ✅
- Gen. B. Chance Saltzman
- Gen. David W. Allvin
- Gen. John D. Lamontagne

> Names change. Saltzman and Allvin are former CSAFs; Lamontagne is the Vice CSAF. Re-check this
> one before every quiz.

**5. When reporting to a superior officer, which action is most appropriate?**
- Knock once, enter and stand at attention, salute, and give the greeting of the day
- **Knock once, enter and stand at attention, salute, and say "sir/ma'am, Cadet Blank reports (as ordered)"** ✅
- Knock twice, enter and stand at attention and greet the officer
- Remain at ease and wait for the officer to approach you

> Script: "Sir/Ma'am, Cadet [Last Name] reports as ordered." On your own initiative, say
> "…reports [to ask a question / make a statement]."

**6. Which situation generally requires a salute?**
- While eating in the dining facility
- Indoors while in uniform when encountering a general officer
- Indoors while wearing civilian clothes
- **Outdoors in uniform when encountering a commissioned officer** ✅

**7. When reporting out from a superior's office, the cadet should:**
- Come to attention, give the greeting of the day and depart
- Turn around and walk out immediately
- **Come to attention, salute, give the greeting of the day and depart** ⚠️
- Remain seated until the superior leaves

> Best guess. It mirrors the reporting-in routine (which has a salute) and is the only option that
> includes one. The exact reporting-out wording was not found in a Det 855 or Det 432 source.

**8. Which of the following is not an appropriate reason to wear the Air Force uniform?**
- While at a PMT event
- While engaging in an approved off campus activity
- **At a fast food establishment following an event that requires you to be in uniform** ⚠️
- While at work following an event that requires you to be in uniform

> Best guess, and a strong one: the other three are authorized wear, and the uniform rules
> prohibit eating at off-base restaurants in OCP. The "fast food after an event" wording itself was
> not found verbatim in a Det 855 or Det 432 source. Confirm with cadre if it comes up worded
> differently.

**9. Are GMC cadets allowed to wear a silver name tag in Class A service dress?**
- Yes
- **No** ✅
- Maybe
- Only if they are above 6 foot

> The silver name tag is for cadet officers (contracted/POC). Non-contracted GMC cadets don't
> wear it on service dress or the pullover sweater. This one is specific to Det 855's own uniform
> guide.

**10. Which statement best describes proper military grooming standards?**
- Grooming standards only apply during inspections
- **Grooming standards apply whenever wearing the uniform** ✅
- Grooming standards are optional for ROTC cadets
- Grooming standards only apply to active-duty personnel

## Week 2

**1. Which of the following is the Air Force mission?**
- To defend the United States through land, sea, and air power
- **To fly, fight and win... Airpower anytime, anywhere.** ✅
- To secure the Nation's interests in, from, and to space
- To build leaders of character for military service

> The space option is the Space Force mission.

**2. What minimum percentage of mandatory standard PMT must a cadet attend to receive a passing grade and remain in good standing?**
- 70%
- 75%
- **80%** ✅
- 90%

> AFROTCI 36-2011, para 9.9.1. 79% or below in any one of AS class, LLAB or PT is an automatic
> failure. An approved excused absence comes out of the total instead of counting against you
> (20 sessions minus 2 excused = 18; you still need 80% of 18, which is 14).

**3. Which type of absence can lead to progressive consequences such as formal feedback, an LOC, and eventually an MCE?**
- Excused and explained (E/E)
- Unexcused but explained (U/E)
- **Unexcused and unexplained (U/U)** ⚠️
- Excused but unexplained (E/U)

> Best guess, by elimination: U/U is the most serious combination. The E/E, U/E, U/U, E/U codes
> and which one triggers the LOC → MCE ladder were **not found written down anywhere**. Ask
> cadre or find the syllabus before relying on this.

**4. What should a cadet do before participating in a high-risk activity such as skydiving or scuba diving?**
- Notify their flight afterward
- **Meet with cadre before participating** ✅
- Only report it if an injury occurs
- Nothing if it is off campus

> The real requirement: a safety brief from the Detachment Safety Officer and the required safety
> paperwork (AF Form 4392) **before** participating.

**5. What is the primary purpose of Form 48?**
- Track PT scores
- Record uniform issue items
- **Show and communicate your academic plan and expected graduation timeline** ✅
- Apply for Field Training

**6. How soon must a cadet report an interaction with law enforcement, including campus police, to cadre?**
- 24 hours
- 48 hours
- **72 hours** ✅
- One week

> This is a Civil Involvement (CI). Report new ones within 72 hours, even during breaks. Changes
> to an existing CI also have a 72-hour clock.

**7. Which statement about international travel is correct for Det 855 cadets?**
- No briefing is required for personal travel
- Only contracted cadets need to notify cadre
- **International travel requires a briefing with cadre** ⚠️
- Cadets only need to notify their flight commander

> Best guess, by elimination and by analogy to the pre-departure safety brief. The requirement
> was **not found written down** for Det 855 or Det 432. Confirm with cadre.

**8. Which of the following lists the three Air Force Core Values?**
- Honor, Courage, Commitment
- **Integrity First, Service Before Self, Excellence in All We Do** ✅
- Duty, Honor, Country
- Character, Connection, Commitment

> "Honor, Courage, Commitment" is the Navy. "Duty, Honor, Country" is West Point.

**9. Which statement is the AFROTC Honor Code?**
- Duty, Honor, Country
- Always place the mission first
- **"We will not lie, steal, or cheat, nor tolerate among us anyone who does."** ✅
- Integrity First, Service Before Self, Excellence in All We Do

> The core values are not the honor code; they are a different thing.

**10. Which of the following is one of the Seven Basic Responses?**
- "I'll try my best, Sir/Ma'am."
- "Probably, Sir/Ma'am."
- **"No excuse, Sir/Ma'am."** ✅
- "I think so, Sir/Ma'am."

> All seven: Yes, Sir/Ma'am · No, Sir/Ma'am · No excuse, Sir/Ma'am · Sir/Ma'am, may I make a
> statement? · Sir/Ma'am, may I ask a question? · Sir/Ma'am, I do not understand. · Sir/Ma'am, I
> do not know.
