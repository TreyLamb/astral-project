Flashcard shows the insignia — say the rank out loud, reveal, move on. Nothing is scored or tracked; it's just a deck to cycle. Pick insignia gives you the name and four insignia to choose from — that one is scored, and anything you get wrong is re-dealt at the end, so a round isn't over until you've cleared it.

Keyboard: Space reveal, then next · 1–4 pick an option · R reshuffle.

Insignia drawn from the AFROTC rank chart. Officer stripes read outboard edge inward; GMC marks are diagonal slashes on the epaulet and chevrons on the utility pin. Note the chart's abbreviations are C/2Lt and C/1Lt — some pubs write these C/2d Lt and C/1st Lt.
