# US Military Officer Ranks

Pay grades O-1 to O-10. Years of service are typical, not fixed.

| Pay Grade | General Years of Service | Army / USMC / USAF / USSF Titles             | Navy / Coast Guard Titles            |
| :-------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| O-1       | 0 - 2 years              | Second Lieutenant (2LT / 2ndLt / 2d Lt)      | Ensign (ENS)                         |
| O-2       | 2 - 4 years              | First Lieutenant (1LT / 1stLt / 1st Lt)      | Lieutenant Junior Grade (LTJG)       |
| O-3       | 4 - 10 years             | Captain (CPT / Capt)                         | Lieutenant (LT)                      |
| O-4       | 10 - 16 years            | Major (MAJ / Maj)                            | Lieutenant Commander (LCDR)          |
| O-5       | 16 - 22 years            | Lieutenant Colonel (LTC / LtCol / Lt Col)    | Commander (CDR)                      |
| O-6       | 22 - 25+ years           | Colonel (COL / Col)                          | Captain (CAPT)                       |
| O-7       | 25+ years                | Brigadier General (BG / BGen / Brig Gen)     | Rear Admiral Lower Half (RDML)       |
| O-8       | 28+ years                | Major General (MG / MajGen / Maj Gen)        | Rear Admiral (RADM)                  |
| O-9       | 30+ years                | Lieutenant General (LTG / LtGen / Lt Gen)    | Vice Admiral (VADM)                  |
| O-10      | 30+ years                | General (GEN / Gen)                          | Admiral (ADM)                        |
