# ACFT 2-Mile Plan — Full Calendar with Example Paces & Times

**Read this note first:** every pace and time below is a *planning estimate*, built by assuming you hit each checkpoint on schedule and interpolating smoothly between them. Real training is never perfectly linear — treat these as reasonable targets, not commitments. **The numbers should be recalculated using your actual checkpoint results as you go**, not followed blindly off this sheet if reality diverges. Where a cycle includes a checkpoint test, that real result should replace the estimate for every cycle after it.

**How daily mileage works:** Days 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, and 10 are your 7 core running days and are the ones that add up to each cycle's "Total mileage" figure from the main plan. Day 9 (hike/ruck or optional short run) and Day 11 (optional mobility/easy jog) are extra — not counted in that total, use them based on how you feel.

## If you're behind or ahead of a checkpoint

- **Behind target at a checkpoint:** don't try to make it up by jamming extra intensity into the next cycle. Recalculate the following cycles' paces off your *actual* new time, and consider stretching Phase 2 by an extra cycle if you're meaningfully behind by Checkpoint 2 — better to shift the finish target than to force it.
- **Ahead of target:** great, but resist the urge to jump paces further ahead than the next cycle calls for. The overload is progressive on purpose.
- **Missed a cycle's mileage due to travel/life/lifting fatigue:** don't try to "catch up" the missed miles — just resume the plan at the current day. Catch-up mileage is a common way overuse injuries happen.
