# MFT calendar: 10 proposed improvements (2026-11-03)

Each PNG shows the calendar as it is now (left, "NOW") beside the proposed design (right), with
what changes, why, and which calendar app does it that way. Nothing here is built yet; these are
proposals to pick from. They are based on Google Calendar (web, incl. its July 2026 density
update) and Apple Calendar (Mac and iPhone).

| # | Proposal | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Day view becomes an hour grid (time axis, blocks sized by duration, all-day band, now-line) | Large |
| 02 | Google-style day headers; the location pill only where it changes | Small |
| 03 | "+N more" instead of clipping, opening a day pop-over | Medium |
| 04 | One event-row design (time · dot · title) for every kind of item | Medium |
| 05 | Four fixed type sizes everywhere | Small |
| 06 | A Density setting (Compact / Comfortable / Responsive) instead of self-resizing chips | Medium |
| 07 | Eleven toggle pills become one Layers menu (doubles as the colour legend) | Medium |
| 08 | A mini-month navigator above Notes | Small |
| 09 | Fade past days/items and draw a "now" line in Week view | Small |
| 10 | Deadlines in a band across the top of the week | Medium |

Several fit together: 04 + 05 + 06 are one typography pass; 10 is the same band as 01's all-day
row; 03 replaces the auto-shrinking that 06 removes.
