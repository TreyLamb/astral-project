# ASVAB Master Study Guide

Consolidated from `asvab_questions.md`, `asvab_study_guide.md`, `quiz_questions.md`, and `testqs.md` (all merged and removed on 2026-07-05). Organized by subject. Every set now has an answer key (2026-07-06 update added keys to every set that originally lacked one — a handful are marked `*` where the source screenshot's numbers didn't cleanly resolve to a listed choice; see the note next to each).

Several sections also got dedicated "whole-topic" sets (2026-07-06) so that studying one fact forces reviewing the surrounding topic instead of just that one fact — e.g., Biology now drills all major cell organelles and the full taxonomy hierarchy (not just "mitochondria = powerhouse"), Physical Science covers all three Newton's Laws plus core physics formulas, and Earth & Space Science covers Earth's full internal/atmospheric layer stack, all major cloud types, the full geologic time scale, and the solar system.

A handful of near-identical questions appear more than once across sets (different sources independently generated similar problems) — these were left in place as extra repetition. Only exact, word-for-word duplicate blocks were removed, including three-plus repeats found in the appendix (see "Duplicates removed" at the end).
